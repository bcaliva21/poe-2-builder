# Modifier language

PoE2 answers fail most often when "increased", "more", and "damage taken" are mixed up.

## Core stacking

- **Increased / reduced** modifiers to the same thing usually **add** with each other, then apply as one combined multiplier.
- **More / less** modifiers usually **multiply** separately (each is its own multiplier).
- **Damage taken** modifiers on the enemy (like Shock) apply on the defender side and are separate from your "increased damage" offensive mods.
- Local item mods (on a weapon/armour) often only affect that item's base stats; global mods affect the character.

## Reading a comparison question

When asked "is A or B better for my build?":

1. Name which buckets A and B enter (hit damage, crit, ailment chance, ailment magnitude, attack/cast speed, damage taken on enemy, defense, uptime).
2. Check caps / soft caps (e.g. shock magnitude cap, crit chance practical ceiling).
3. Check whether the character already stacked one bucket heavily (diminishing additive returns).
4. Check whether the mechanic even applies (no lightning → shock chance from hits may be irrelevant without conversion/support).

## Confidence rule

If you cannot place a mod into a bucket with a source citation, do not pretend precision. Quote the mod text and reason qualitatively.
