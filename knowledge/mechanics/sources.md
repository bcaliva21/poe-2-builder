**Pinned content window:** PoE2 early-access mechanics as commonly documented through 2025-2026 community/wiki sources. Treat in-game tooltips and current patch notes as higher priority if they conflict.

## Data policy

| Source | Use for |
|---|---|
| `knowledge/mechanics/` | Formulas, caps, stacking rules |
| `knowledge/reference/` | Concrete gems/items/mods when present |
| Session `character.json` | The player's current stats |
| Model memory | Last resort only; label as unverified |

## When uncertain

Say so. Prefer asking for a PoB stat or an in-game tooltip over guessing. After major patches, re-verify ailment, crit, and defense chapters first.
