# Defenses

Keep offense and defense comparisons honest: a DPS upgrade that makes you die is often not "better" for real clears.

## Common layers

- **Life / Energy Shield** - raw pools
- **Resistances** - elemental mitigation; respect caps
- **Armour** - physical hit mitigation (with whatever current PoE2 armour curve the patch uses)
- **Evasion / deflect-style avoidance** - chance-based or hybrid avoidance depending on patch wording
- **Block / spell block** - chance to negate or reduce hits if present
- **Damage reduction / less damage taken** - carefully separate from enemy "increased damage taken"
- **Recovery** - regen, recharge, leech, flasks/charms, on-kill

## Advice pattern

1. Identify the death cause implied by the user (one-shots, dots, ailment, bosses, maps mods).
2. Prefer the layer that addresses that failure mode.
3. Quote current session defenses when available (life, ES, res, ehp-like PoB figures).
4. Do not invent exact mitigation % from armour/evasion without a formula citation or PoB export value.

## Offense vs defense questions

If the user asks only about damage scaling, answer the damage question, then optionally note obvious squish risk if the session shows very low pool/resists. Do not derail every DPS question into a tank lecture.
